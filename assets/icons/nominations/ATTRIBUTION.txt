Nomination icons are adapted from Iconoir v7.12.2 (regular SVGs), with a shared FLARE accent color and optical stroke weight. The football/soccer-ball, gamepad, crown, dice, group, calendar, heart, mask, emoji, rocket, sparkles, star, flame, chat, and thumbs-down symbols use Iconoir. The pistol, crossed-swords, and clapperboard silhouettes are custom SVGs drawn to match Iconoir's 24×24 outline style.
Source: https://iconoir.com/ · https://github.com/iconoir-icons/iconoir

The voted-status check and the category-card trophy/medal remain adapted from Hugeicons Core Free Icons v4.4.0.
Source: https://hugeicons.com/icons
