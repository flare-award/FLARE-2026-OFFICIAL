All nomination and voted-status icons are adapted from Hugeicons Core Free Icons v4.4.0.
Source: https://hugeicons.com/icons
The original 24×24 Hugeicons paths and native 1.5-unit stroke settings are preserved; only the color is customized (#f2cf80 for nominations, #d4ffc5 for the vote check). Native rounded line caps and joins are kept.
The Vibe of the Year nomination uses Hugeicons Sparkles; Best CS uses Crosshair; Best Rematch uses Football Pitch; Breakthrough of the Year uses Rocket 02; Clip of the Year uses Film 01; the event calendar is the blank Calendar 04 variant.
