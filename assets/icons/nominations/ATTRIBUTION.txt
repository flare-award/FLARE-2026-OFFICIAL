The nomination and voted-status SVG icons in this directory are adapted from Tabler Icons Outline, @tabler/icons v3.50.0.
Source: https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons
Copyright (c) 2020-2026 Paweł Kuna.

The SVGs are normalized to a 64×64 viewBox, with consistent round caps/joins and 1-unit Tabler stroke; nomination symbols use #f2cf80, while the vote-confirmation check uses #d4ffc5. The rocket retains the Tabler geometry previously supplied by the user. `mood-xd` is used as the closest playful Tabler face for the meme nomination; `movie`, `crosshair`, and `swords` are the nearest Tabler equivalents for clip, CS, and conflict themes.
