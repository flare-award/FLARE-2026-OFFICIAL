Active nomination-card icons now use the rounded filled Phosphor Icons v2.1.1 set, recolored with a restrained FLARE gold-to-bronze gradient. Phosphor symbols used: crown, game-controller, circles-four, soccer-ball, smiley-sticker, rocket, film-slate, heart, mask-happy, calendar, users-three, sparkle, star, fire, chat-circle-dots, thumbs-down, and sword (composed into crossed swords).
Source: https://phosphoricons.com/ · https://github.com/phosphor-icons/core

The CS `pistol.svg` uses the Font Awesome Free Solid `faGun` glyph, v7.3.1, adapted with the same gradient.
Source: https://fontawesome.com/icons/gun?f=classic&s=solid · https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Unused legacy Iconoir SVGs are retained under the Iconoir MIT license below.

The voted-status check and the category-card trophy/medal remain adapted from Hugeicons Core Free Icons v4.4.0.
Source: https://hugeicons.com/icons
