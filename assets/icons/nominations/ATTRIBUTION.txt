All nomination and voted-status icons are adapted from Hugeicons Core Free Icons v4.4.0.
Source: https://hugeicons.com/icons
The original 24×24 Hugeicons paths are preserved with rounded line caps and joins. Nomination icon strokes are adjusted to 1.0 units for a lighter look; the vote check retains its original weight and color (#d4ffc5). Nomination icons use #f2cf80; the two category-card icons use their matching accent colors.
The Vibe of the Year nomination uses Hugeicons Heart; the Style of the Year nomination uses Hugeicons TShirt; Best CS uses Crosshair; Best Rematch uses Football Pitch; Breakthrough of the Year uses Rocket 02; Clip of the Year uses Film 01; the event calendar is the blank Calendar 04 variant. The category cards use Hugeicons Trophy and Medal 01.
